The Hidden Crisis

The Dangerous Reality of Free Online Converters

When candidates upload their documents to random web converters, they unwittingly surrender their most sensitive identity assets to unknown servers.

✕

Free Cloud Converters

  • ⚠️
    Biometric Left Thumb & Signature Harvesting: Handwritten signatures and LTI scans are easily scraped and vectorized to forge bank cheques, unauthorized loan agreements, and legal deeds.
  • ⚠️
    Aadhaar & PAN Card KYC Identity Theft: Leaked identity cards provide threat actors with the complete details required to construct synthetic identities and open fraudulent micro-finance accounts.
  • ⚠️
    SIM-Swap & OTP Diversion Fraud: Attackers use leaked candidate identity proofs at telecom retail counters to hijack duplicate SIM cards and bypass bank 2FA OTPs.
  • ⚠️
    Indefinite Retention in Public Cloud Buckets: Uploads often sit unencrypted in open Amazon S3 buckets or temporary server folders, exposed to web crawlers and dark-web scrapers.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How to resize an image to 50 KB or 20 KB? ↓

Open Dastavez Studio, drop your image or document, and set your target file size (e.g. 20 KB – 50 KB). The iterative binary search compression engine automatically adjusts JPEG quality factors and chroma subsampling in milliseconds to guarantee your file meets the exact portal limit without blurry pixelation.

How do I resize a photo to 200x230 px or signature to 140x60 px? ↓

Select the Exam Document Studio or Image Tools tab, pick your preset (e.g. IBPS, SSC, or UPSC), or enter custom dimensions (200×230 px for passport photos, 140×60 px for signatures). The interactive live preview workbench lets you inspect aspect ratio and sharpness before exporting.

Can I batch resize multiple images at once? ↓

Yes. The built-in Batch Processing studio allows you to drag-and-drop dozens or hundreds of photos simultaneously. It uses bounded multi-threaded CPU workers to resize, compress, and convert images to JPG, PNG, or WebP at high speed.

Does Dastavez Studio require an active internet connection? ↓

No. Dastavez Studio is 100% offline and air-gapped. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi, turn off Ethernet, or work in remote areas with zero connectivity. All image resizing, PDF compression, OCR, and AI models execute strictly on your device.

Are my Aadhaar, signatures, or photos stored on your servers? ↓

We have zero servers! We do not collect, transmit, or cache your files anywhere. Everything runs in ephemeral volatile RAM buffers on your local computer, which are automatically purged when you close the application.

Is Dastavez Studio really completely free? ↓

Yes, 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, no watermarks, and no annoying advertisements.

What Windows operating systems are supported? ↓

It natively supports Windows 10 (version 1809 / build 17763 or newer) and all versions of Windows 11 on x64 architecture.

How does the built-in update mechanism work? ↓

Inside the app (in Settings or About), clicking "Check for Updates" queries the public GitHub Releases API. When a new version is released, you can download and apply the upgrade seamlessly with a single click.
